# Roadmap

What's next, in order, then the backlog and open questions. When an item is taken,
its rules go into `docs/DESIGN.md` and the item leaves this file. Player-facing prose
and the score are authored, and a9 ratifies them.

The direction: **know, commit, lose your grip, and an honest accounting.** The player
gets data and owns the synthesis; the game never draws the conclusion. The core loop is
form a view, accept exposure, revise under consequence. Each round answers a
player-facing test: after one quarter, can a player name the evidence they used, the
exposure they chose, and what remains unresolved?

## Next: one decision chain, end to end

A demonstration and its dispute are built. The rest of the chain, in two rounds:

1. **Commitment.**
   - A conditional financing commitment: anchor Polaris's next round if an independent
     evaluation lands first. A mandate has an owner, a deadline, reserved resources,
     release conditions and a real cost for withdrawing.
   - Retire the terminal clip (it keeps `d_W`'s sign, so the player can never carry a
     world across the holds line, and their leverage is zero at the knife-edge). Bound
     influence where it is exercised: available capital, implementation delay,
     diminishing returns per channel, competing institutions, the opportunities offered.
   - Recalibrate the knife-edge on effective outcomes with matched-policy headless runs.
   - Must hold: reserved resources can't be spent twice; conditions can't be met
     retroactively; every enacted commitment has an attributable channel.
2. **Disclosure and the firm.**
   - Spending the edge, for any evidence the player holds: trade on it privately,
     circulate it inside Meridian (moves `F`, not `B`), brief a partner, publish through
     Tan (closes the gap, ends the trade, strains the source), or make a public call.
   - The public call enters `B` weighted by the caller's frozen credibility, and above a
     threshold can pull forward certain minor public events (a Polaris round, a
     demonstration, a follow-on, a listing, the Street's echo). Crossings,
     certifications, settlements, incidents and families stay out of its reach; a call
     traded against draws regulatory exposure.
   - Insider exposure read from fills, not from the verb clicked.
   - The firm responds: conversion changes how the desk operates; scrutiny gets
     "explain this thesis" dispatches and forced trims.
   - Pins on a thesis, and a terminal receipt of what was known, bet, promised and
     disclosed.

Then generalize: an evidence record with public and private views, mandates as the
fund-as-actor menu, Meridian as a firm on its page, an AI-policy lobbying menu in place
of the party-PAC bar, and a room that offers what the player's preparations made
feasible.

## After that, in order

- **What the book bets.** For each open rung and the coming quarter, the book's value
  if it certifies and if it doesn't, beside the draft's and the market's probability.
  Later a Kelly-equivalent reading (never "the owner's belief"), once the valuation
  covers complete wealth per outcome, and "follow my book" as a forecast policy.
- **The accounting.** The last edition separates recorded contributions, model
  counterfactuals (first: the world without you, a stated intervention-free policy
  replayed headless) and unresolved attribution. Around it: the reader's family head at
  each act boundary beside what happened, the sampled variables disclosed, and the
  run's editions reprinted with what was true in the margin.
- **Delegation of judgment.** Obligations and mandates coming due in Act III as its
  affirmative objective; the licensed reader (Halcyon's model on the Street, its share
  of `B` the η-weight; no τ-keyed tilt in its forecast); the Brief's own editor
  ghostwritten by rung; a regime-switch wrapper so the pricing tools stop describing the
  market; halts with their settlement.
- **Domains**, when players want the distinction: one hidden scalar per lab, with each
  domain (mathematics, software, AI research, cyber and bio, persuasion) certifying on
  its own clock and listed per domain. Needs a refit.
- **Shared runs:** shared seeds, a daily edition, the last edition as a keepsake.
  Worlds shared across browsers need portable math.

## Backlog

- **Instruments:** AI catastrophe bonds on detected S2+ incidents (an S4 cannot pay);
  selling liquidity against public block requests; rolling rung listings and event
  contracts. Clearing machinery (a regime-aware quote surface, an exchange variance index,
  stressed-scenario limits) when a feature needs it.
- **The race:** security levels with costs and tells; self-exfiltration; world-side heat
  impulses; a post-theft margin burn; detection driven by transparency and media;
  open-ecosystem dynamics (family 4's proliferation variant is unreachable today); pauses
  and withheld checkpoints; new incident classes; adversarial persuasion.
- **Content:** satire of the state (supervision as certification infrastructure, a
  lab-drafted preemption bill, Beijing's Sputnik response to a demonstration); an
  illustration pilot in its own session, a contact sheet for a9 to choose from.
- **Continuity:** a Zhaowei model named "Qilin-4" (the line is Cangjie); Liang Wei
  called "Zhaowei CEO" (he is the Premier); real firms named in `src/events/market.js`
  and `halcyon.js`; an Algorithmic Capability Disclosure Act nothing activates; the
  epilogue's "Alignment" stat row; the retired LLM-mode storage cleared on every boot.
- **Seams:** dead `tick()` in `main.js`; the step control says "one day" and steps a
  substep; touch on the curve editor is untested; race-page labels collide when events
  fall within weeks.

## Open questions

- The book-move stop fires too often on a levered desk (7 times in 40 days on the
  catalyst's book); scale it to the book's own volatility? Waits on a9's play-test.
- At about 29% of act boundaries the reader's family head gives one family more than
  0.60, against the knife-edge's target of none.
- Does `K_CRED` 1.35 feel generous or stingy to a person? How do the posterior editor,
  the valuation gaps (a parity claim gaps HCN about 13%), the rounds' cadence,
  `ACT3_TUNING` and the standing orders' lock at R5 feel in play?
- The reader's known limits (pre-claim cohorts misread within a day; V drifting
  predictably at about 0.027 a quarter) point to a nonlinear model class over the same
  features. Take it when a round needs a tighter measuring stick.
- The political pool: trim how often it leads the paper before deleting content.
- Further out: other seats on the same race (Treasury, Tan's newsroom, Halcyon's board).
