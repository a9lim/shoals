# 04 — Narrative Arcs

Event content. Arcs run on the event engine's primitives — Poisson draws
with `when` guards, followup chains, one-shots, recurring pulses,
portfolio triggers — plus **ledger firing**: whatever the race model
decides (releases, detections, certifications, thefts, the summit
window and its outcome) fires from the race ledger through the race
bridge, never from a Poisson draw. Arc guards key on the public released
frontier rung (`world.ai.frontierRung`). Constants and rulings are in
[02a-tuning.md](02a-tuning.md).

## Act tempo (the AI 2027 cadence trick)

Chapters cover less time as capability compounds. Mechanically: the
discretionary event base rate scales linearly in the released frontier
rung (×1.0 at R1 → ×2.5 at R5), and from released R4 discretionary events
arrive on substeps, not just at day close — the day stops being the
atomic unit of history exactly when it stops being one in-fiction.

**Standing orders.** Tempo compression must not mean more popups. At
released R3 the Desk offers six precommitments to arm: de-risk on a
grave incident, halve the HCN stock on a blockade, bonds go first in a
liquidation, harvest in-the-money binaries on a certification, sit on
every tip, flatten an 8% day. They lock at R5. Precommitments execute as
machinery while the player's own orders slow on a latency ladder as the
rung climbs: takeoff is felt as your own agency migrating into machinery
you configured earlier — the desk's version of history outrunning the
human decision cycle.

**Seeding rule: the hints start on day one.** Act I is not quiet — it's
*unheard*. Severity-0 incidents played for comedy, a "Fixedpoint"
half-sentence on page four of the Brief, cadence arithmetic that never
decelerates, a model-designed molecule clearing Phase I below the fold.
Everything Act III makes deafening should be audible in Act I to a
player who is listening, and invisible to one who isn't. First
playthroughs miss it; second playthroughs can't stop seeing it. That
asymmetry is the replay hook and the game's honest claim about the real
world.

## Core arcs

**The release ladder** (every lab). Releases are the race's metronome,
and the rung claims that ride them are the discourse's; Consensus
settles on rung crossings, not vibes ([02-race-model.md](02-race-model.md)).
Aleph keynotes (with a whisper of the internal build and a "buy the gap"
call), point releases, Cangjie drops that are capability and
proliferation at once and rally the non-frontier complex while hitting
HCN, certifications, and Halcyon's cadence arithmetic — the *cadence
itself* is evidence the player can trade and the market initially
refuses to read. Each rise of the released frontier rung steps long
rates (the capex wave) and is a Halcyon follow-on offering.

**Fixedpoint.** A half-sentence on page four of the Brief while the
frontier is young; the codename's leak, a superevent once the released
frontier reaches R3; afterward Halcyon's self-graded Fixedpoint Index,
which Consensus declines to list — a claim with a decimal point.
Halcyon's voluntary pause ends "for some work", and the next release
ships on the cadence the pause was meant to interrupt.

**The incident ladder** (the genre: capability × reduced safeguard ×
mundane human error — see [02-race-model.md](02-race-model.md)).
Escalating severity, constant anatomy, three wings: frontier accidents,
misuse via refusal-stripped Tianxia fine-tunes, and — past R3 — the
persuasion class. Incidents occur silently and are detected on a lag,
or never; the market moves on detection, the damage was done at
occurrence. A detection is followed by the lab's post-mortem and then a
scoped independent review — thorough, careful, and bounded to exactly
the perimeter the lab drew around it. From severity 2 up the unit is a
population of agents, not one model. An S4 announces itself and ends
the run.

**The insider channel** (the game's moral core). A safety-researcher
source network grown through standing: tips arrive from undetected
incidents, gated on safety-network trust and throttled to one per 45
days — the channel is a person, not a feed. A tip says *something
happened* at a named lab, not filed, possibly never filed. Three verbs
per tip — **trade it, leak it (Rachel Tan), sit on it** — with distinct
signatures: trading costs regulatory exposure and leaves a flag a
subpoena reads best; leaking forces the incident's detection within
days, raises media trust and strains the network; sitting deepens the
network. Sources are characters with followup chains: the correction
call, the outcome beat.

**The evidence ladder** (the alignment twin of the incident ladder).
Technical evidence beats with sampled reliability, found versus
published, τ-correlated and never τ-identifying. It runs beneath the
feed and folds into the market's alignment sentiment; it has no beat of
its own on the wire.

**The wonders track** (the pull side of the wager). A model-designed
molecule clears Phase I below the fold in Act I and is approved a
quarter later; materials and growth prints follow; a proof is claimed,
disputed, and then verified; by R4 the cascade arrives in the same feed
as the incidents, at the same compounding tempo, driven by the same
variable. Wonders move HCN in the same direction a melt-up would, and
every wonder is *evidence* — of exactly what, the game declines to say.
The ambivalence rule lives here: utopia stays one step away,
indistinguishable from the hazard until it is too late to matter. If
the wonders ever stop reading as slightly ominous, or the incidents as
slightly miraculous, the mix is mistuned.

**The ghostwritten world** (AI in the discourse, not just the market).
At R3 the memos start reading like the model wrote them, because they
do — the CIO's quarterly letter grows bullet points, everyone's prose
says "delve". At R4 the government flail turns abruptly *competent* —
loopholes close, the czar's testimony parses — precisely when the memos
stop being human: the satire register's last joke and the straight
register's first warning. Beneath it run the persuasion-class
incidents, quiet at occurrence, a scandal on detection.

**The Polaris schism.** A scaling decision goes the wrong way; the
walkout is a superevent; Gottlieb's first interview goes to Tan; Polaris
comes to the desk as a compute-starved supplicant and then slips a
milestone while its alumni get hired away. Polaris raises private rounds
from three weeks after the walkout, so backing the careful racer is a
position. Conscience, competitor, supplicant — and, in some worlds, the
margin-carrier that matters at resolution.

**The treaty track** (the Reykjavik Framework — AI 2040-shaped).
Verification talks surface early and die of farce in a Geneva working
group. Mid-game, compute-reporting groundwork (player-lobbyable) keeps
the door ajar, and the racers write to Washington asking for the tools
to stop *later*. Late in the term: the window — one live negotiation at
Reykjavik, opened regardless of whether a deal was ever possible, and
failing if a grave incident lands during summit week. Whether an
implemented treaty holds is `chinaTrue.dealPossible`'s secret. Mostly
it fails, and the failure should feel like the world's fault, not the
dice's.

**China in the dark.** Export controls (a popup that raises the control
stage) against the domestic Tianxia constituency — American firms
lobbying against their own government's controls; Beijing's reciprocal
answer; Cambria allocation politics in two capitals; the guards-versus-
GPUs memo; intelligence reads on Beijing's velocity, each right only
some of the time — the player's China posterior is as tradeable as
their timeline. The weight theft is load-bearing: the exfiltration that
erases a lead overnight, disclosed later or never, with attribution —
spies, an insider, or the model itself — sampled and disputed. **And
the strait:** Cambria's fabs sit a hundred miles from the coast Beijing
drills off of; gray-zone scares and blockades are recurring
risk-premium machinery, and in some runs the tail fires. Losing the
race conventionally makes the unconventional option more attractive —
the market prices the coupling before the Pentagon says it.

**The firm.** Act I: the skeptical CIO and the scrutiny loop. Then the
grudging memo — "walk me through the thesis" — once credibility opens
the memo gate and `F` has begun to wake; then the verdict, conversion or
not. Hedging the memo cannot stop a firm that the evidence converts, but
it costs the attribution (the verdict needs a higher `F`). The person
who thought you were crazy, deciding out loud how much crazy the
evidence now licenses. Around it, the desk: Webb's risk allocation,
Vasquez's patronage, Riggs at the next screen.

**The political world** (satire register: pressure on the race, not the
plot). The Barron administration and its Congress, the Fed under
Hartley, and the scandals and wars that compete with the race for
attention — and losing that competition *is* the satire. The Bowman
affair grows from offshore accounts to an indictment; a Gulf war
(Operation Dustwalker) and a Hormuz closure ladder; a covert operation
in Bolivia; a Russian border crisis; tariffs and a trade war; the
midterms; an impeachment. The government's race apparatus flails in
one-liners — an AI czar appointed for loyalty, an evals office funded
then subpoenaed, export rules that ban chips and rediscover, in public,
that weights don't un-release, a Russian border flare answered by post,
an OPEC cut that matters because datacenters eat power. Occasionally the
flail lurches into consequence, and the market moves more on the noise
than the signal — which is itself a lesson the player can monetize.

## The room (endgame branch point)

One decision event, AI 2027's branch rendered as earned access: when
the leader lab reaches the final scaling decision, *whether the player
is present* — and what weight their voice carries — is a pure function
of accumulated standing: credibility, safety-network trust, lab
relations, the firm's conversion, what the player knew and backed, the
Treasury backchannel. High-standing runs get the terrible privilege:
advise speed, advise margin, advise the deal (only while a summit window
is live), or say nothing — one voice among several, never a control
knob. The room is influence, not authorship; hidden state still resolves
the world. You bought a seat, not the steering wheel. It convenes once,
the first day the leader's internal track is within 0.15 of R5; below
the seat gate the player is not called, and the ending arrives as
weather, on the news. The gate and voice arithmetic are in
[02a](02a-tuning.md).

## Cross-domain wiring

The causal web the arcs are written against. Links through the dials
and the ledgers are mechanized ([02](02-race-model.md),
[02a](02a-tuning.md)); links marked † are carried by event prose only.

- Incidents → control-regime pressure → supervision, mobilization →
  Consensus freezes, the nationalization trade looms
- Incidents → the market moves on detection; undetected incidents → no
  backlash → margin illusion → `S` mispriced by everyone, including the
  player
- Incident-reporting regime → detection lag ↓ → the insider edge thins —
  the player can lobby the world safer and their own edge thinner
- Classification → incident detections go dark → the insider channel is
  the only feed
- Tianxia release → `C[tianxia]` ↑ and the `heat` floor ↑ (permanent) →
  non-frontier rally † and a stronger constituency † → export controls
  harder to pass †
- Export controls → Tianxia compute growth ↓ → Halcyon lead ↑ — the game
  quietly rewarding hawkishness with safety margin, which is the
  reluctant-accelerationist trap working as intended
- Strait scare → compute-curve backwardation (blockade) and the `heat`
  overlay; export-control politics sharpen †
- Weight theft → lead evaporates → `S` accumulation stops race-wide →
  mobilization politics; the treaty dies or gets suddenly serious †
- Certifications and claims → `B` → HCN's valuation gaps and the
  compute lift — the market waking up
- Wonders → HCN melt-up impulses (the market believes for the nice
  reason) — the ambivalence rule as wiring
- Leaks → Tan's credibility ladder → public pressure †
- Everything → the ledger ([05-endings.md](05-endings.md) complicity
  accounting)
