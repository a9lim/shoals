# 06 — Naming

The settled name slate and the rules behind it. The lore rule — **real
places, fictional people** — is stated in [01-world.md](01-world.md).

## Principles

1. **Existential register, not critique register.** Names sound like what
   the entities believe about themselves, not like the author's editorial.
2. **Bland-real corporate texture for companies and products; mythic
   weight reserved for model lines.** Internal codenames may be
   on-the-nose — real ones are.
3. **No real-company puns** (the Palanthropic problem).
4. **Versionable model names** — "X 4.5" must read naturally in a
   MarketWire headline.

## The navigation conceit

The game is *Shoals* — the hazard on the chart. Everyone names themselves
after ways of not running aground, and nobody agrees on where the rocks
are:

- **Shoals** — the game. The hazard itself.
- **Meridian Capital** — the fund. The line you navigate by.
- **Polaris** — the safety lab. The fixed star you steer by: distant,
  dim in bad weather.
- **Halcyon** — the frontier lab. The one *non*-navigation name in the
  set, deliberately: the kingfisher that nests on the open sea, which
  grows calm *because it is nesting*. The others try to steer past the
  rocks; Halcyon believes that while it is building, there will be no
  storm. Its own hubris, stated as a brand.

## The slate

**Halcyon** — frontier lab, San Francisco. Products as straight as the
real world names things: **Halcyon Chat** (consumer), **Halcyon Teammate**
(enterprise agents — chillingly ordinary on purpose), **Halcyon Compute**
(platform). The recursion product is **Project Fixedpoint** — internal
codename register, mathematically on-the-nose exactly the way real
codenames are: the model that produces the model, x = f(x). "Fixedpoint"
becomes a household word when it leaks.

**Aleph** — Halcyon's model line. Borges's point that contains all
points; the transfinite ladder (ℵ₀, ℵ₁, …) is quietly the whole subject;
versions cleanly ("Aleph 4.5"). History: 1.x research, 2.x first
commercial, current line in production at game start. The collision with
the real-world Aleph Alpha is known and accepted.

**Tianxia** — Zhaowei Technologies' frontier program ("all under
heaven"), Shenzhen; the open-weights player (see
[01-world.md](01-world.md)). Model line **Cangjie** — the four-eyed
legendary inventor of Chinese characters. A language model named for the
man who made language writable; real-world-plausible (the Cangjie input
method exists); open-weights artifacts read right: "Cangjie-3-235B".

**Polaris** — the safety splinter, Boulder. Model line **Holt** — a
small sheltered wood, and an otter's den: the warm, defensible,
deliberately *small* place. The racer names its model after the totality
of all infinities; the safety lab names theirs after a den. That contrast
is thesis-bearing, and "Holt 2" reads perfectly in a headline.
Deliberately not Walden: right register, but "Walden 2" collides with
Skinner's utopian novel, a resonance the game doesn't control.

**Cambria Semiconductors** — the chip chokepoint, Hsinchu (keeps the
Cambrian-explosion shadow at plausible-corporate arm's length). CEO
Helena Karras.

**HCN / VXHCN** — the game's one chart underlying is Halcyon common
(HCN); VXHCN is the single-name vol index on it, the way real
single-stock vol is watched for the giants (see
[03-market-mechanics.md](03-market-mechanics.md)).

**Consensus Markets** — the prediction-market venue for milestone
binaries. Bland-real. Absolutely not "Delphi."

**Geography** — the United States (Washington), China (Beijing; Zhaowei
in Shenzhen), Taiwan (Hsinchu — Cambria's fabs, and the strait's standing
tail), Russia, OPEC/the Gulf (the Strait of Hormuz), Bolivia as texture.
Meridian Capital in New York; Halcyon in San Francisco; Polaris in
Boulder. Foreign policy outside the U.S.–China axis is one-liners, Taiwan
excepted (see [01-world.md](01-world.md)); Israel is just Israel if ever
needed.

**Parties stay fictional** (Federalist / Farmer-Labor) — the one
deliberate exception to the real-places-real-institutions rule: real
party names would read as partisan commentary rather than structural
satire, and the F / F-L architecture (seats, filibuster, midterms) is
load-bearing in the engine.

## Dead names

These belong to the politics-satire version of the game and encode the
shallow critique; runtime files carry none of them, and new content is
never written against them: Palanthropic/PNTH, Silmarillion, Atlas
Sentinel/Aegis/Companion/Crucible, Covenant, Aletheia, Ptonos, Hearth (a
separate open-weights lab is redundant with Tianxia's identity), Pharos,
Lodestar, VXPNT (and `vix` in identifiers — the vol index is VXHCN), and
the fictional geography: Federal States of Columbia, Serica (`serica*` —
the field is `chinaRelations`), Khasuria, Farsistan, Boliviara, Meridia,
Phoenicia.
