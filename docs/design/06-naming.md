# 06 — Naming

The name slate and the rules behind it. The lore rule — **real places,
fictional people** — is stated in [01-world.md](01-world.md).

## Principles

1. **Existential register, not critique register.** Names sound like what
   the entities believe about themselves, not like the author's editorial.
2. **Bland-real corporate texture for companies and products; mythic
   weight reserved for model lines.** Internal codenames may be
   on-the-nose — real ones are.
3. **No real-company puns.**
4. **Versionable model names** — "X 4.5" must read naturally in a
   MarketWire headline.

## The navigation conceit

The game is *Shoals* — the hazard on the chart. Everyone names themselves
after ways of not running aground, and nobody agrees on where the rocks
are:

- **Shoals** — the game. The hazard itself.
- **Meridian Capital** — the fund. The line you navigate by.
- **Polaris** — the safety lab. The fixed star you steer by: distant,
  dim in bad weather.
- **Halcyon** — the frontier lab. The one *non*-navigation name in the
  set, deliberately: the kingfisher that nests on the open sea, which
  grows calm *because it is nesting*. The others try to steer past the
  rocks; Halcyon believes that while it is building, there will be no
  storm. Its own hubris, stated as a brand.

## The slate

**Halcyon** — the frontier lab. Products as straight as the real world
names things: **Halcyon Chat** (consumer) and **Halcyon Compute**
(platform). The recursion program is **Project Fixedpoint** — internal
codename register, mathematically on-the-nose exactly the way real
codenames are: the model that produces the model, x = f(x).
"Fixedpoint" becomes a household word when it leaks.

**Aleph** — Halcyon's model line. Borges's point that contains all
points; the transfinite ladder (ℵ₀, ℵ₁, …) is quietly the whole subject.

**Tianxia** — Zhaowei's frontier program ("all under heaven"), Shenzhen;
the open-weights player (see [01-world.md](01-world.md)). Model line
**Cangjie** — the four-eyed legendary inventor of Chinese characters. A
language model named for the man who made language writable, and
real-world-plausible (the Cangjie input method exists).

**Polaris** — the safety splinter, Boulder. Model line **Holt** — a
small sheltered wood, and an otter's den: the warm, defensible,
deliberately *small* place. The racer names its model after the totality
of all infinities; the safety lab names theirs after a den. That
contrast is thesis-bearing.

**Cambria** — the chip chokepoint, Hsinchu (the Cambrian-explosion
shadow kept at plausible-corporate arm's length). CEO Helena Karras.

**HCN / VXHCN** — the game's one chart underlying is Halcyon common
(HCN); VXHCN is the single-name vol index on it, the way real
single-stock vol is watched for the giants (see
[03-market-mechanics.md](03-market-mechanics.md)).

**Consensus** — the prediction-market venue for milestone binaries.
Bland-real.

**Geography** — the United States (Washington; the Austin fab; Boulder),
China (Beijing; Zhaowei in Shenzhen), Taiwan (Hsinchu — Cambria's fabs,
and the strait's standing tail), the treaty track's Geneva, Singapore
and Reykjavik, and the world the administration is distracted by: the
Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz, Israel, Russia, Bolivia.

**Parties stay fictional** (Federalist / Farmer-Labor) — the one
deliberate exception to the real-places-real-institutions rule: real
party names would read as partisan commentary rather than structural
satire, and the F / F-L architecture (seats, filibuster, midterms) is
load-bearing in the engine.

## Retired names

Names from earlier versions of the game appear nowhere at runtime and
are never written against: the Palanthropic/PNTH lab and its products,
the VXPNT index (and `vix` in identifiers), the separate open-weights
lab Hearth, and the invented polities (Columbia, Serica, Khasuria,
Farsistan, Boliviara, Meridia, Phoenicia).
